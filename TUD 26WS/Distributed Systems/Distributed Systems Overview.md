## Learning outcomes

After completing the course, you should be able to:

- Analyze a distributed application's requirements and translate the analysis into a practical solution.
- Analyze system behavior in context and identify problems.
- Apply fundamental concepts and technologies to design solutions.
- Select appropriate concepts and technologies and justify design decisions.
