## Distributed Software Architecture
### Role of Architectural Design
- System architecture is formed at an early stage of the development process
- Incremental development of architectures is usually not successful since refactoring a system architecture is expensive
- An architectural model indentifies the main structural compoenents and their interraltionships
### Programming in the Large vs in the Small
**Programming in the large (handling the forrest)**
- Knitting the modules together into an integrated whole with a module interconnection language.
**Programming in the small (handling the trees)**
- Implementation of the individual modules with a typical programming language.
### Architectural Design Decisions
- Series of structural decisions dependent on the type and specific requirements of the system to develop.
- Principle of architectural decomposition - components with sub-components at multiple levels of hierarchy.
- Usually based on an architectural pattern
