## Dependability
**Definition**: Dependability reflects the trustworthiness of a computer system. Trustworthiness means the degree of confidence a user has that the system will work as they expect.
**Four principal dimensions to dependability**
- Availability
- Reliability
- Safety
- Security
## CAP Theorem
**Definition**: The trade-off between Consistency, Availability and Partioning tolerance.
You can have only two out of three:
- Consistency
- Availability
- Partioning Tolerance
Partitioning tolerance is a must since partitioning can't be avoided.
- Either consistency or availability
