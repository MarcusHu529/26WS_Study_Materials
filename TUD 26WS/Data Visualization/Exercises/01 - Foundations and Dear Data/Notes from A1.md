2.1 VisLies:

    BTC Visualization:
        issues: non-uniform polygon area used for quantitative + too much text (two values) 
        how-to-improve: less text, pie chart + legend, or even better: barchart

    Public vs Private Sector: 
        issues: two uneven y axes scales, no obvious mapping
        how-to-improve: only one y axis, fair comparison (eg starting at zero)

2.2 Perception and color: 
    Gestalt Principles: 
        Similarity, proximity, closure (and a bit symmetry)

    Color Scales / Rainbow Color Scale: 
        issues: cyclic, not ordered, poor contrast, unnatural in several regions. 
        however, it can be useful for some cases. If you want to learn more, check out: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0924271622002659 
        
        better scales: it always depends on the data! 
        in general, high lightness contrasts = good color scale. For example, Greyscale, Parula, Viridis...
        for more than 2 (extrema) colors in a scale, you may want to consider a different visual variable.
