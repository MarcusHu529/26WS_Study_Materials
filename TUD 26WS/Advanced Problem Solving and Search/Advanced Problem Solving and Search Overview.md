# Advanced Problem Solving and Search

Winter Term 2026/2027 · TUD

## Course information

- **Abbreviation:** APSS
- **Focus:** Techniques for solving difficult problems in artificial intelligence
- **Teaching language:** English
- **Format:** Lectures, tutorial exercises, self-study, and a written examination
- **Registration and materials:** [OPAL course](https://bildungsportal.sachsen.de/opal/auth/RepositoryEntry/56319377408)
- **Lecturer, teaching assistants, and rooms:** Not specified in the supplied course information

This course does not cover machine learning or neural networks. It was previously named **Problem Solving and Search in Artificial Intelligence (PSSAI)**. The contents of PSSAI and APSS are identical; students may take only one of the two courses.

## How this course folder works

- **Lectures:** Numbered syllabus topics. Keep each topic's notes and lecture materials together. Numbers indicate syllabus order, not teaching weeks. Topic-specific self-study material stays with its topic.
- **Exercises:** One numbered folder per tutorial sheet, containing the sheet and any solutions you write. This uses the same Exercises section as the other courses.
- **Exam Preparation:** Revision summaries, practice questions, and exam information when available.
- **Resources:** Supplementary material used across several topics.

Future topic folders are prepared but empty; an empty folder does not indicate study progress.

## Learning outcomes

- Identify why typical AI problems are difficult to solve.
- Analyze algorithms and methods for AI problems and recognize when to apply them.
- Understand connections between a problem's graph structure and its complexity, and methods for tackling it.
- Analyze problems and develop solutions in the tutorials.

## Prerequisites

- Basic knowledge of theoretical computer science and logic
- Good English skills; teaching is exclusively in English
